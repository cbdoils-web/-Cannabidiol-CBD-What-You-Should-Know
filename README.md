# -Cannabidiol-CBD-What-You-Should-Know
CBD is a natural compound found in cannabis and hemp plants that does not produce the intoxicating “high” associated with THC. It has gained attention for its potential therapeutic applications, and research continues to explore its benefits and limitations. However, not all CBD products are the same, and quality, dosage, possible side effects, and drug interactions matter. 💡 Stay informed, check product quality, and consult a healthcare professional when needed.
